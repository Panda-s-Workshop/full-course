Modpacks and config

Configuration, compatibility with other mods, and everything a pack can change with data.

Configuration

The settings live on the server and are sent to each player when they join, so tooltips, the meal row and the meal slots always match the server.

  • NeoForge: config/fullcourse-synced.toml, created with the defaults and a comment for each key on first start.
  • Fabric: config/fullcourse.properties, created the same way. It is read once at startup.

Restart the server (or the game, in single player) after editing either file. NeoForge's in-game config screen marks these keys the same way: they take effect on the next world load.

The natural_health_regeneration gamerule works as in vanilla: turned off (as UHC packs do), nothing heals on its own, neither Recovery nor food. Healing potions, golden apples and other effects still work.

KeyDefaultWhat it does
keepVanillaHungerfalseKeep the vanilla food bar and regeneration. Meals still give their benefits, except Mending: with Recovery off it has nothing to speed up (tooltips say so).
recoveryDelaySeconds8Seconds without taking damage before health starts coming back (0–600).
recoveryIntervalTicks80Ticks between heals of 1 HP, before meal benefits shorten it (0–12000; 0 turns Recovery off, and food still heals).
startingMealSlots2Meal slots a new player has (1–16).
maxMealSlots5The most meal slots a player can have (1–16). The Tasting Journal opens three past the starting slots; /fullcourse unlock can go up to this.
keepMealsOnDeathfalseKeep held meals through death.
durationMultiplier1.0Multiplies every meal's duration (0.05–20).
tierThresholds1,3,5,8,12Lowest recipe-chain score of tiers 1 to 5: exactly five, ascending, 1–999. Anything else falls back to the defaults.
slotMilestones8,15,20Different dishes to taste for meal slot 3 (two stars or more), 4 and 5 (three stars or more; slot 5 also needs a feast). Exactly three, each 1–9999, and slot 5's not below slot 4's; anything else falls back to the defaults. Raise them in a pack with many food mods.

Each player also has three display settings of their own, which the server never sees: config/fullcourse-client.toml on NeoForge, config/fullcourse-client.properties on Fabric. Restart the game after editing.

Compatibility

Full Course rates every food in the game from its recipes, including foods from other mods, so it needs no list of supported mods. Machine recipes that don't list their ingredients the usual way (Create's, for instance) are read from their own fields where they can be. Recipes it still can't read are counted in one line of the log at server start, by recipe type; a food made only that way rates as a raw ingredient until a pack rates it (see below). Some mods get more than that:

  • Farmer's Delight Refabricated (Fabric): its dishes get tiers from their cooking-pot and cutting-board recipes (a cut such as minced beef or bacon is still a raw ingredient, but the cut counts toward every dish made from it), its knives work on the Cutting Board, and Full Course's ingredients carry the shared c: tags.
  • JEI: a recipe category for each kitchen station. Hover the Cooking Pot's arrow for what the grid makes, or click it for all pot recipes.
  • AppleSkin: its hunger overlays and tooltips are hidden, since there is no food bar to show them on. With keepVanillaHunger on they stay.
  • Cooking for Blockheads: the Cooking Table lists Full Course's crafted dishes and ingredients and can sort them by tier, and its oven heats a Cooking Pot set on top while the oven is cooking something.

For pack makers

Everything below is data, so a data pack changes it; /reload re-rates every food.

Rating a food by hand. data/<namespace>/fullcourse/food_profiles/<item path>.json rates the item <namespace>:<item path>. Every field is optional; what's left out comes from the recipes. An empty object {} puts a replaced file back to the scored values.

{"tier": 4, "families": ["brawn", "vigor"], "remedy": false, "serving_of": "minecraft:cake"}
  • tier: stars, 0 to 5 (0 gives no meal).
  • families: any of vigor, brawn, fortitude, swift, diligence, fortune, tide, night_eye, ember, lightfoot, mending. A name not in this list is logged and skipped; an empty list leaves the families to the recipes.
  • remedy: true for foods like the golden apple, which keep their own effects and heal but give no meal.
  • serving_of: the dish this is a serving or slice of (a feast block, a pie); it rates exactly as that dish.

Full Course's own dishes are rated by files at these paths too (data/fullcourse/fullcourse/food_profiles/<dish>.json, and cake's in data/minecraft/), which set their families and leave the tier to the recipes. A pack file at one of those paths replaces the whole file: a file with only tier leaves the dish's families to its recipes, so copy the families to keep them. A food in fullcourse:soups gets Mending only when its families come from its recipes; families set in a file are used as written.

What the scorer can't see. Fluids a machine pours in (Create's filling, for instance) aren't ingredients; only items are. Another mod's tool that a crafting recipe uses and gives back (a rolling pin, a knife) counts as an ingredient unless it is in fullcourse:containers, and a non-edible intermediate (a flour, a paste) ends the chain as a raw ingredient unless it is in fullcourse:culinary: tag them there. The recipe lines a tooltip shows with Shift are Full Course's own recipes as shipped; a data pack's changed recipes rate correctly but don't change those lines.

Item tags (namespace fullcourse):

TagWhat it means
raw_ingredientsObtained raw (crops, drops, fruit): always the start of a recipe chain, never a meal.
culinaryNot edible, but followed down its recipes as an ingredient (flour, sugar). Anything edible already is.
containersBowls, bottles, buckets: ignored as ingredients.
remediesTheir own effects and the heal only, no meal.
placed_mealsPlaced and eaten as a block (feasts, cake): their score gets +3. A dish named only by a serving_of override (another mod's feast block) gets +1.
soupsAlways give Mending.
groups/<family>The ingredients that give each benefit family to foods rated from their recipes. Full Course's own dishes, Grilled Fillet, Flatbread and cake have their families set in their food profiles, so these tags don't change them.
journal_foodsThe "any simple dish" the Tasting Journal's recipe takes.
repairs_flint_knifeWhat mends a Flint Knife on an anvil (flint).

Block tags: fullcourse:heat_sources heat a Cooking Pot set on top; it holds vanilla's #minecraft:campfires and #minecraft:fire, so other mods' campfires count. A block with a lit or burning state heats only while that state is on; any other block in the tag always heats. fullcourse:heat_conductors (a hopper, so a pot can be emptied from below) pass heat up from a source under them.

Effect tag: fullcourse:needs_hunger lists effects that act only through the food bar (Farmer's Delight's Nourishment and Comfort). With hunger off they do nothing, and tooltips grey them out and say so. Vanilla Hunger isn't in it: here it is Queasy, which stops new meals from starting and pauses Recovery.

Biome tags: fullcourse:has_wild_onions and fullcourse:has_wild_ember_peppers choose where the wild crops grow.

Shared tags. Recipes take #c:eggs, #c:foods/dough, #c:foods/cooked_egg, #c:foods/raw_meat, #c:foods/raw_fish and #c:crops/onion, so other mods' items work in them. The mod puts its onion, ember pepper, dough, fried egg, grilled fillet and flatbread in the matching c: tags.

Recipe types, for data packs:

TypeFields
fullcourse:cuttingingredient, result (Cutting Board, one knife use)
fullcourse:cookingingredients (up to 6), optional container the dish is served in, result, optional time (ticks, 1 to 32767, default 200), optional soup_color ("#RRGGBB")
fullcourse:bakingingredients (up to 4), result, optional time (1 to 32767, default 300)
fullcourse:fermentingingredients (up to 4), result, optional time (1 to 1000000, default 12000; sleeping counts)

Every other mod's recipe type counts too: each type is its own station in a dish's score.
